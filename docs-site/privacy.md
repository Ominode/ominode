# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: July 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ominode ("we", "us") collects when you use ominode.com and the Ominode API (the "Service"), how we use it, and the rights you have. It applies worldwide; additional rights for EEA/UK and California residents are described in sections 7 and 8.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account data**
- Email address, username, password hash, and (if you use them) third-party login identifiers (GitHub, Discord, Google, Telegram, etc.).

**Billing data**
- Top-up amounts, balance, transaction history, and redemption-code usage. Card numbers and bank details are handled by our payment processors (e.g. Stripe); we receive only confirmation metadata (last four digits, payment status, processor transaction IDs).

**Usage data**
- API request metadata: timestamp, model requested, token counts, cost, latency, status/error codes, and a request ID.
- IP address, browser/device information, and approximate location derived from IP, for security and abuse prevention.

**Content data**
- Prompts, files, and other input you send through the API ("Input"), and the model output returned to you ("Output"), which transit our systems as necessary to relay them to third-party AI providers ("Upstream Providers").

**Support data**
- Messages you send us when requesting support.

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Provide the Service**: authenticate you, relay API requests to Upstream Providers, meter usage, and debit your balance.
- **Billing and accounting**: process payments, prevent double-spending, maintain legally required financial records.
- **Security and abuse prevention**: detect fraud, credential abuse, bots, multi-account farming, and violations of our Terms.
- **Operate and improve**: monitor performance, debug errors, and understand aggregate usage.
- **Communicate**: send transactional messages (verification, password reset, receipts, security and service notices). We do not send marketing email without consent.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your API Input to train our own models.

## 3. How Content Flows to Upstream Providers

When you call the API, your Input is transmitted to the Upstream Provider that operates the model you selected. That provider processes the Input under its own privacy policy and data-processing terms. We select providers and configurations that do not use API traffic for model training where the provider offers such terms, but we do not control Upstream Providers' systems. Do not submit third parties' personal data through the API unless you have a lawful basis to do so.

## 4. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we process personal data on these bases:

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Providing the Service, billing | Performance of a contract |
| Security, fraud and abuse prevention | Legitimate interests |
| Financial record-keeping | Legal obligation |
| Optional communications | Consent |

## 5. Sharing

We share personal data only with:

- **Upstream AI providers** — your Input, as described in section 3;
- **Payment processors** — to process your payments;
- **Infrastructure providers** — hosting, CDN, and email delivery vendors acting as processors under contract;
- **Authorities** — where required by law, subpoena, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

- Account data is kept while your account is active.
- API request **metadata** (tokens, cost, timestamps — not prompt content) is retained for billing, auditing, and abuse investigation for up to **[12] months**.
- Prompt/Output content transits the Service and is not retained beyond what is technically necessary to deliver the response, except where retained briefly for abuse investigation or debugging.
- Financial records are kept as required by tax and accounting law.
- You may request account deletion (section 7); deletion removes your profile and credentials, subject to the retention duties above.

## 7. Your Rights (EEA/UK and elsewhere)

You may request: access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing, and data portability. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise rights, contact us at **[support@ominode.com]**. We respond within the time required by applicable law (normally 30 days under GDPR).

## 8. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. California residents may request to know, access, correct, or delete their personal information, and may not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Submit requests to **[support@ominode.com]**.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard measures: TLS encryption in transit, hashed passwords, encrypted storage of API credentials, access controls, and audit logging of administrative actions. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

## 10. International Transfers

We process and store data in **[Singapore / the regions of our infrastructure providers]**. Where data is transferred from the EEA/UK to countries without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) where required.

## 11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their data.
